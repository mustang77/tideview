Reel 01 — Powertrain

Torque, placed by the wheel

Nothing here is borrowed. The pack is the floor, the floor is the chassis, and every wheel gets its own argument about grip.

One front / two rear

Three motors

Axial-flux units spinning to 22,000 rpm. The rear pair act independently, so the car rotates on power instead of waiting for the brakes.

118 kWh structural

The floor is the battery

Cells bond directly into the monocoque. That removes a pack case, 61 kg, and the flex that used to sit between your seat and the road.

1,050 kg at 250 km/h

Downforce, no wing

A full-length venturi tunnel does the work a rear wing usually does, and keeps the silhouette flat enough to hit 402 km/h.
