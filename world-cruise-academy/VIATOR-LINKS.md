## Homepage "Before You Sail" buttons
- **Airport & port transfers** → Viator (tracked, `campaign=transfers`).
- **Cruise travel insurance** → parked (`#`). Viator does NOT sell insurance;
  needs a separate affiliate program (e.g. VisitorsCoverage, SafetyWing,
  Travelex). Sign up, then swap `#` for that link.

## Tracking
Viator sets the affiliate cookie when a visitor lands via these links; a booking
within the cookie window is credited to you. Check earnings in the Viator
Partner dashboard → Performance, filterable by the `campaign` SubIDs above.
