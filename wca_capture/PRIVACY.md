# Privacy Policy — WORLD CRUISE ACADEMY Screenshot & Screen Recorder

**Effective date: August 27, 2026**

## Summary

This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any user data.
Everything it does happens locally on your computer.

## What the extension does with data

- **Screenshots** you capture are processed entirely in your browser. They are
  handed from the capture step to the local editor page and exist only on
  your device. You decide whether to download or copy them.
- **Screen recordings** are created in your browser's memory and saved only
  when you choose to save them, only to your own computer.
- **Settings** (none currently beyond a temporary hand-off of the captured
  image) are stored using Chrome's local extension storage on your device.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not collect personal information of any kind.
- We do not transmit any data to any server. The extension makes no network
  requests.
- We do not use analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising identifiers.
- We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
- We do not read your browsing history or monitor the pages you visit. The
  extension only accesses the page you explicitly choose to capture, at the
  moment you click it.

## Permissions explained

- **activeTab** — one-time access to the tab you clicked the extension on, so
  it can take the screenshot you asked for.
- **scripting** — draws the area-selection rectangle on the page when you use
  "Capture selected area".
- **storage** — passes the captured image to the local editor page.

## Changes to this policy

If the extension's behavior ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this
document will be updated and the extension's store listing will reflect it.
