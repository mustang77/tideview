# Placement Interview Tips

How recruiting agencies interview cruise ship hotel staff, and how to pass. #placement #interview

The interview is short, in English, and tests three things: attitude, communication, and experience. Typical questions:
- Tell me about yourself.
- Why do you want to work on a cruise ship?
- Describe a time you handled an angry guest.
- How do you feel about working seven days a week for six to eight months?

Answer in two or three sentences, smile, and give a real example from a hotel or restaurant job. Wear a white shirt and dark trousers. Bring the full [[Crew Documents Checklist]].

Contracts for first-time waiters are usually six to eight months on, two months off. Salary details are in [[Contracts and Salary]].
