# Crew Documents Checklist

Documents every Indonesian crew candidate must have ready before a contract offer. #documents #placement

- Passport valid for at least 18 months.
- Seaman's book (Buku Pelaut) issued by the Indonesian maritime authority.
- STCW basic safety training certificate: personal survival, fire fighting, first aid, personal safety and social responsibility. See [[Ship Safety Basics]].
- Medical certificate from an approved clinic, including vaccinations required by the cruise line.
- Police clearance certificate.
- Reference letters from previous hospitality employers.
- C1/D visa application for United States itineraries, and a Schengen visa for European joining ports.

Keep scanned copies of everything in one folder. Recruiters reject incomplete files. Interview preparation is in [[Placement Interview Tips]].
