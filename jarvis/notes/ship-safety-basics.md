# Ship Safety Basics

Every crew member must know the general emergency signal: seven short blasts followed by one long blast on the ship's whistle and alarm. On hearing it, go to your muster station wearing your life jacket. #safety #training

The muster list on the back of your cabin door shows your emergency duty and station. New crew attend a safety induction within 24 hours of joining and a drill within the first week.

Common duties in an emergency:
- Guide guests to their assembly station calmly.
- Close fire doors and watertight doors when instructed.
- Count guests at the assembly station and report to the station leader.

The STCW basic safety training certificate is required before joining. See [[Crew Documents Checklist]].
