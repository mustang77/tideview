# Restaurant Service Sequence

The standard sequence of service in a cruise ship main dining room. #service #restaurant

1. Greet and seat within one minute of arrival.
2. Present menus and offer water, then take drink orders within three minutes.
3. Take the food order, repeat it back, and enter it in the point of sale system.
4. Serve bread and drinks before the appetizer.
5. Serve courses from the right, clear from the right, ladies first.
6. Check back within two minutes of serving each course.
7. Offer dessert and coffee, then present the guest with the farewell.

Assistant waiters handle bread, water, clearing and resetting. Waiters own the order, the timing and the guest relationship. Phrases are in [[Guest Service English]].
