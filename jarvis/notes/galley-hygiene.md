# Galley Hygiene

Public health rules on cruise ships are strict because a single outbreak can end a cruise. Ships sailing from the United States are inspected under the Vessel Sanitation Program. #hygiene #galley #safety

Golden rules for anyone touching food:
- Wash hands for 20 seconds before handling food, after breaks, and after touching your face.
- Report any illness before your shift. Sick crew are isolated, never sent to work.
- Keep cold food below 5 degrees Celsius and hot food above 63 degrees Celsius.
- Label and date everything. First in, first out.
- Never leave food uncovered on a counter.

Buffet attendants also police guests: replace serving utensils, keep sneeze guards clean, and offer hand sanitiser at the entrance. Fire safety in the galley follows [[Ship Safety Basics]].
