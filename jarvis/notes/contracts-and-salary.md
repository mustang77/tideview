# Contracts and Salary

What a first contract looks like for hotel department crew. #contract #salary #placement

A first contract is typically six to eight months on board followed by six to ten weeks of vacation. Cabins are shared by two crew. Food, accommodation and uniform are provided.

Entry pay ranges, in US dollars per month, as of the academy's latest placement data:
- Galley utility and buffet attendant: 900 to 1,300.
- Assistant waiter: 1,200 to 1,800 including gratuities.
- Waiter: 2,000 to 3,200 including gratuities.
- Bar staff: 1,500 to 2,500 including gratuities.

Gratuities depend on guest ratings, which is why [[Guest Service English]] and the [[Restaurant Service Sequence]] matter so much. Most lines pay by bank transfer at the end of each month.
