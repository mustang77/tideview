# World Cruise Academy

World Cruise Academy is a cruise ship crew training school in Yogyakarta, Indonesia, founded by Yoga, a former server on Virgin Voyages with more than ten years at sea. #academy #overview

The academy prepares Indonesian candidates for hotel department jobs on cruise ships: waiter, assistant waiter, bar staff, cabin steward, galley utility and buffet attendant. Training covers English for guests, ship safety, hygiene, and the interview process with recruiting agencies.

Programs run in small batches. Every candidate completes [[Ship Safety Basics]], [[Guest Service English]], and [[Crew Documents Checklist]] before the placement interview described in [[Placement Interview Tips]].
