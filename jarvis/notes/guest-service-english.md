# Guest Service English

Guests expect warm, confident English. The academy teaches a small set of phrases that cover most restaurant and bar situations. #english #service

Greeting: "Good evening, welcome to the restaurant. My name is Yoga and I will be taking care of you tonight."
Taking an order: "May I take your order?" and "How would you like your steak cooked?"
Handling a complaint: "I am very sorry about that. Let me fix it for you right away."
Closing: "Is there anything else I can get for you? Enjoy your evening."

Practice tips: speak slowly, smile, keep eye contact, and repeat the order back to the guest. Guest names matter: use them after the first introduction.
