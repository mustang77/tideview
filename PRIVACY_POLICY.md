# Privacy Policy — Al-Quran

_Effective date: 28 July 2026_

Al-Quran ("the app") is a Quran reading, listening and prayer-times
application. Your privacy matters; this policy explains exactly what the
app does with data.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app has no user accounts, no registration, no analytics, no
advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, store, or
share any personal information on our servers — we operate no servers.

## Data stored on your device only

The app keeps your preferences locally on your device and nowhere else:

- Display name (optional, only if you enter one)
- Bookmarks and last-read position
- Translation, reciter, theme and text-size settings
- Prayer-times city and country
- Tasbih (dhikr) counter value

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of this data. None of it
leaves your device except as described below.

## Network requests

The app requests content from these third-party services:

| Service | Purpose | Data sent |
|---|---|---|
| AlQuran Cloud API (alquran.cloud) | Quran text, translations, search | Requested surah/search terms |
| Islamic Network CDN (islamic.network) | Recitation audio | Requested verse |
| Aladhan API (aladhan.com) | Prayer times and Hijri date | The city and country name you type |
| Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com) | Amiri and Inter fonts | Standard font file requests |

These requests are ordinary content downloads (like a web browser
fetching a page). We do not attach any identifiers to them. Each service
has its own privacy policy.

## Permissions

The app requests only the **Internet** permission, used solely for the
content above. It does not access your location, contacts, camera,
microphone, or files.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone,
including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this
